## The entry

> Note to self: the snake was not a garden hose.

## Why this line

A tombstone is the one place where a note to yourself is completely useless — and that is exactly what makes it funny. The man spent his final breath writing a memo he will never read, about a mistake he will never get to correct. The joke lands in the last four words: the garden hose bit back. One short line, no explanation needed, and the kind of line you repeat to a friend the moment you read it.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes the epitaph as a single line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`), with no trailing newline. A guard enforces the bounty rules: 1–150 characters, a single line, non-whitespace.
